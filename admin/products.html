📖 Variable Product Manual & Guide

💡 1. What is a Variable Product in Fireworks?

A Variable Product is a single firecracker catalog item that comes in multiple variations (such as lengths, counts, shots, or packaging sizes). Rather than cluttering your store with separate listings for each size, customers choose their desired option directly from a single product page.

Common Sivakasi Fireworks Examples:
  • Sparklers: 10cm, 15cm, 30cm, 50cm Jumbo lengths.
  • Wala Garlands: 100 Wala, 1000 Wala, 5000 Wala, 10000 Wala counts.
  • Sky Shots: 12 Shots, 25 Shots, 50 Shots, 120 Shots cakes.
  • Gift Boxes: Standard (25 pcs), Deluxe (50 pcs), Jumbo (75 pcs).
🛠️ 2. Step-by-Step Manual to Add Variable Products
  1. Click "+ Add New Firecracker" button on the top right.
  2. In the Product Structure switch, select "Variable Product".
  3. Enter the general Firecracker Name (e.g. "Classic Sivakasi Electric Sparklers") and choose its Category.
  4. In the Variant Builder, you can either:
    • Click a Quick 1-Click Preset (e.g. Sparkler Lengths, Wala Garlands, Sky Shots).
    • Or click "+ Add Variant Row" to manually type your own variant options.
  5. Set individual SKU, Selling Price, MRP, and Stock Quantity for each variant.
  6. Paste your Image URL and optional YouTube Demonstration / Video Guide link.
  7. Click "Save Product".
🛒 3. How Customers Experience Variable Products

On the public storefront, variable items display a "From ₹XX" or price range badge. On clicking into the product, customers see sleek option pills (e.g. [ 10cm - ₹35 ] [ 15cm - ₹85 ]). Selecting an option updates the price, SKU, stock indicator, and adds that specific option to the cart and invoice.

In the POS Billing Terminal, clicking a variable product opens a 1-tap touch selector for the cashier to choose the exact variant for the receipt.
