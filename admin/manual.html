⚡ 1. POS Billing Counter & Terminal

SPEED BILLING

The POS Billing Counter is engineered for rapid high-volume customer checkout during peak Diwali rush hours without any lagging or cloud delays.

1
Product Quick Search & Selection
Type product name, SKU (e.g. SPK-10, CHK-DLX), or click any category pill to filter. Click on any product card or tap the Add button to add it to the active bill.
2
Variable Products Selection (Packs & Sizes)
If a product has multiple options (e.g. 10cm, 15cm, 30cm or 12, 25, 50 shots), a popup will appear allowing you to select the exact variant and quantity in 1 tap.
3
Customer Information & WhatsApp Number
Enter the customer's 10-digit mobile number and full name. The system automatically verifies previous festival purchase history and points balance.
4
Payment & 1-Click Thermal Print
Select Cash, UPI (Instant Dynamic QR), or Card. Click Complete Sale & Print to produce an 80mm or 58mm thermal receipt with the store logo and immediately send the bill to the customer's WhatsApp!
💡
Festival Pro-Tip: Press F2 anytime to jump immediately to the product search bar, or press Enter to add the highlighted item.

✨ 3. Variable Products & Option Presets

MULTI-VARIANT

Variable products allow multiple variants (e.g. sparkler lengths, wala lengths, or aerial shots) under a single parent listing with independent prices and inventory counts.

1
Select "Variable Product" Type
In the Add/Edit Firecracker modal, select Variable Product (Multiple Sizes / Shots / Variants).
2
Apply Built-in 1-Click Presets
Click any preset button:
  • Sparklers Preset: 10cm, 15cm, 30cm, 50cm Jumbo
  • Aerial Sky Shots Preset: 12, 25, 50, 120 Shots
  • Garlands (Wala) Preset: 100, 1000, 5000, 10000 Wala
  • Chakkar Sizes: Special, Deluxe, Giant, 4x4 Whistling
3
Live Demonstration / YouTube Video Link
Paste a YouTube or Web video link into the Instruction / Video Guide Link field. The storefront will embed this video directly on the product's details page for customers to preview the firecracker burst!

🛠️ 4. Bulk Catalog Tools & Actions

INVENTORY

Manage hundreds of firecrackers at once without repetitive manual edits:

✓
Select All or Check Specific Items
Click the header checkbox to select all filtered firecrackers, or check individual rows. A floating bulk action bar will slide up from the bottom of your screen.
🗑️
Bulk Delete
Deletes selected items from both local and cloud storage with safety confirmation.
🏷️
Bulk Move Category
Re-assign all selected firecrackers to any of the 14 official Diwali categories in 1 click.
📦
Bulk Stock Status (In-Stock / Out-of-Stock)
Instantly mark items as in-stock or out-of-stock across the counter and online storefront.

🧾 5. Thermal Invoices (80mm & 58mm) with Logo

RECEIPTS

Thermal bills are optimized for Bluetooth and USB thermal POS printers with crisp, smudge-free typography and embedded brand logos:

🖨️
80mm Standard POS Printer Format
Full-width 3-inch format with store logo, tax breakdown, customer WhatsApp, itemized variant details, savings highlight, and bilingual festive greeting (Happy Diwali! / இனிய தீபாவளி நல்வாழ்த்துக்கள்!).
🏷️
58mm Compact Bluetooth Mini Printer Format
Ultra-compact 2-inch format featuring high-contrast monocrome store logo, concise 2-column pricing, and quick QR code for instant UPI payment verification.
📄
A4 GST Tax Invoice
Standard full-page format for corporate, wholesale, or bulk orders with full GSTIN credentials, terms of delivery, and authorized signatory seals.

📲 6. WhatsApp Dispatch & Sharing

INSTANT NOTIFY

No manual copying required! Send itemized festival bills directly to your customer's WhatsApp:

1
Automatic Phone Formatting
Entering 9876543210 automatically prepends the India country code 91.
2
Universal Deep Link Dispatch
Clicking Send via WhatsApp opens the native WhatsApp application on mobile devices or WhatsApp Web on desktop computers without popup blocker warnings.
3
Itemized Message with Digital Invoice Link
The generated message includes order number, itemized firecracker summary, total savings, paid status, and a direct link to view and download their digital receipt.

☁️ 7. Offline Resilience & Cloud Sync

ZERO DOWNTIME

During festive sales peaks, internet connections can drop. The system includes dual offline-first architecture:

💾
Instant Local Counter Persistence
Every bill, customer record, and inventory change is instantly committed to browser offline storage. Billing will never freeze even if the internet is completely disconnected.
🔄
Automatic Supabase Cloud Synchronization
As soon as the internet connection resumes, all pending offline bills and inventory changes automatically synchronize with the Supabase cloud database in real time.
